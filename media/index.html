Full Bio About 135 words, for features and event programs

JoAnne Andrea Gomez, RN, is a veteran intensive care nurse, healthcare policy consultant, and founder of ProRN Inc. With more than 27 years of high-acuity experience across Trauma-Neuro and Medical-Surgical ICUs in Toronto, she connects frontline clinical realities with health system economics. She has presented detailed policy frameworks for a Provincial Nursing Float Pool and a Virtual Nursing Program to Ontario health leadership. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and English from the University of Nebraska, active nursing registration in Ontario (CNO) and Nova Scotia, and ICH GCP E6(R3) certification. JoAnne is the author of two books: Burnout to Balance: Your 8-Step Guide to Thriving in a Messy Workplace and Stronger Than You Think: A Guide for Teens Overcoming Anxiety and Childhood Trauma. She writes regularly on healthcare and workforce psychology at medium.com/@msjag416.
