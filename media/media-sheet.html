Full (about 135 words). JoAnne Andrea Gomez, RN, is a veteran intensive care nurse, healthcare policy consultant, and founder of ProRN Inc. With more than 27 years of high-acuity experience across Trauma-Neuro and Medical-Surgical ICUs in Toronto, she connects frontline clinical realities with health system economics. She has presented detailed policy frameworks for a Provincial Nursing Float Pool and a Virtual Nursing Program to Ontario health leadership. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and English from the University of Nebraska, active nursing registration in Ontario (CNO) and Nova Scotia, and ICH GCP E6(R3) certification. JoAnne is the author of two books: Burnout to Balance: Your 8-Step Guide to Thriving in a Messy Workplace and Stronger Than You Think: A Guide for Teens Overcoming Anxiety and Childhood Trauma. She writes regularly on healthcare and workforce psychology at medium.com/@msjag416.

Press Soundbites (cleared for use with attribution)

On Private Agency Spending“Ontario hospitals paid private staffing agencies $725 million in a single year, and Canada-wide the bill has passed $1.5 billion, while permanent bedside lines are cut. That is not a funding scarcity issue; it is a structural misallocation of public health dollars.”
On Nurse Retention“Training new nurses without fixing bedside working conditions is like pouring water into a leaky bucket. In Ontario, 35 of every 100 new nurses leave the profession before they turn 35, nearly double the rate of a decade ago.”
On Workplace Burnout“Workplace burnout is not an individual resilience failure. It happens when dedicated professionals are forced to operate in broken systems that make safe care impossible.”
